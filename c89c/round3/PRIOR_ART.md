# Prior art and attribution

These are candidate, unrefereed results. No priority or optimality claim is made.
The release audit re-read the official table, the unavailable main 89b/89c pages,
and all 47 open PR changed-file inventories on 3 November 2026 UTC. C89 changes
were confined to #270/#276 (K4), #277, #279, #307 and #308. No stronger applicable
K5 upper or K6 lower bound was located in that bounded audit.

- [Jeetu SK, #277](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/pull/277): K5
  step graphon based on the PPSS 192-vertex Cayley graph. The public replay
  comment by Carlos Toledo reports 1,248 changed block-pair entries among
  18,528 optimization variables.
- [Jeetu SK, #279](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/pull/279): K6
  page and fractional upper construction, building on Franek and Deza–Franek–Liu.
  This upper construction is distinct from the new lower-bound method.
- [#307](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/pull/307): the preceding
  three-layer K5 graphon, exact density 2405659597/1427496763392, below 0.00168523.
- [#308](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/pull/308): the preceding
  K6 lower candidate, printed as 0.000025987; its table gives 0.000025987181439.
- [Parczyk, Pokutta, Spiegel and Szabó](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04036), and
  their [certificate archive](https://zenodo.org/records/6602512): published
  Cayley-graph and flag-algebra foundations, including the K5 lower bound.
- [Razborov's flag algebras](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548307008156): the
  sum-of-squares framework. [Kiem–Pokutta–Spiegel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08049)
  report an uncertified K5 lower numerical value, not a released exact bound.
- Thomason's constructions are credited for the folded-Hamming/Cayley family;
  Conlon, de Caen and Mackey for the elementary/general lower-bound baselines.
  Erdős, Erdős–Szekeres, Jagger–Šťovíček–Thomason, Franek and Deza–Franek–Liu
  retain the historical credit recorded in the proposed pages.

Public repository searches sorted by update time, the named AI/agent result
repositories, recent arXiv papers and bounded public community searches supplied
no stronger applicable bound. This is not an exhaustive absence claim. The local
release report and audit receipts retain precise URLs, timestamps and limitations.
