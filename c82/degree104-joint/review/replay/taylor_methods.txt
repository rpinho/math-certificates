Independent integration derivation (implemented in check.py):
For a panel center t0, z0=exp(2*pi*i*t0), angular halfwidth h, and
w(delta)=exp(i*delta)-1, write
 log|z0 exp(i delta)-r| = log|z0-r| + Re sum(k=1..K)
  (-1)^(k+1) (z0/(z0-r))^k w(delta)^k/k + remainder.
If rho=h/|z0-r| < 1, |remainder| <= rho^(K+1)/((K+1)(1-rho)).
Weights and all root enclosures are carried as Arb balls. The signed
sum of coefficients retains cancellations between factors. Its interval
range proves the sign on the whole panel; unresolved sign panels use
0 <= max(0,g) <= max(0,upper(g)), or are bisected.

The averaged moments of w(delta)^k on [-h,h] are real. Using
sum_j (-1)^(k-j) binom(k,j) j^n = k! S(n,k), they equal the series
sum_even_n>=k (-1)^(n/2) h^n k! S(n,k)/(n! (n+1)).
After n=L the absolute error is at most
2^k exp(k*h) (k*h)^(L+1)/(L+1)!.
All coefficients are exact integers/rationals. No cancellation-prone
subtraction of nearly equal sine values is needed.

If a root prevents the log series, elementary ball bounds on root
distances provide an upper bound on g, allowing negative singular
neighborhoods to be discarded only after proving g <= 0 throughout.
Numerator logs may be -infinity there; their upper bounds are finite.
Denominator root moduli are proved different from 1 before integration.
