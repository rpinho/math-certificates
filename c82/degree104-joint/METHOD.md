# Frozen round-12 search method

The construction is Doche's, as stated in PROOF.md. Search is heuristic; only exact frozen-certificate interval replay supplies the bound. Credit: C. Doche supplies the perturbed-polynomial construction (J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux 13 (2001), 103–110). V. Patankar (@vidigoat, #280) supplies the iterated LLL numerator search as well as the weighted-evaluation LLL / closest-vector method, public v3/v4/v5/v6 certificates and coefficient bank. Ricardo Pinho supplies the earlier dual-weighted LLL numerator work [Pin26] (PR #229, [pinned contribution](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/tree/d27a71ff70b57af6e529e6016f741b5cd7dc6f4d)), joint LP, reduced-cost pricing and residue-class restriction. Grinsztajn and Épi supply earlier factor libraries. Credit snapshots include sources/pr280.json, sources/pr301.json and prior_sources/pr280-final.json.

Round 12 of this search started from the union of two exact certificates: my round 11 (certified 0.2519695579*, never published; evidence/round11.json) and V. Patankar's #280 v6 (0.2519694883*; evidence/v6.json), whose pinned MIT data is inputs/v6.json. Both replayed with verify.py before any search. The common D = 740384615402384615382720 represents both sets of normalized weights exactly. Exact modular coprimality checks covered 2,622,668 imported numerator–denominator pairs. v6 contributed 16 new numerators of degrees 24 to 42 on top of numerators from earlier #280 versions, and its six degree-104 denominators are this PR's round-10 denominators. My bank had 22,212 numerator factors and 118 denominators.

The joint LP uses the inherited N=524288 half-circle midpoint grid, HiGHS simplex with primal and dual tolerances 1e-10, and full-bank reduced-cost pricing; all search prices used freshly solved merged duals. The LP is solved in floating point, then multiplicities are rounded to exact integers and exponents to exact rationals, and the resulting certificate is verified. No floating-grid objective certifies a bound. The full-bank union certified an upper endpoint of 0.2519694777811065577145, about 1.05e-8 below the exact v6 replay; this is the combination of my columns with #280 v6's.

The alternations then searched numerators with iterated dual-weighted evaluation-lattice LLL in degree bands 24–48 and 48–64 (seeded by active factors and the v6 additions), with direct coefficient perturbations around the v6 degree-42 factor and the six substitutions generated by X → 1−X and X → 1/X priced as prospective columns. Denominators were searched by closest-vector enumeration at degree 104 on the current merged dual, and one degree-116 probe (priced 354 evaluations, no negative reduced cost) was dropped. Every proposal faced ordinary LP pricing and exact interval replay. Seven alternations ran; only alternation 2 produced a material gain (1.9289e-09, from the degree-36 numerator N82r12_59d304ca3db3), and the search stopped after five consecutive alternations gaining less than 1e-9. Search records are in REPORT.md, COMPUTE.json, rounds/alternations.json and evidence/. No pricing or CVP optimality claim is made.

The producer ran four bank workers and used 2.21 timed worker core-hours over 1.13 wall hours; this packaging job runs no search. The final certificate has 6 monic degree-104 denominators (generated in rounds 8, 9 and 10 of this search) and 38 numerators, 10 of them non-monic. Exact v3/v4/v5 overlaps are zero denominator and five numerator factors (P1, P2, P3, P4, P6) for each version; the exact v6 overlap is six denominators and 30 numerators. Full provenance for every factor is in README.md, FACTOR_PROVENANCE.json and receipts/certificate-facts.json. None of these provenance labels is a novelty claim.

The separately written (same agent) producer verifier, verify.py, and the clean-room referee checker enclose the final bound; their scope and caveats are in PROOF.md, README.md and review/. No third-party Python is copied, imported or executed. Public coefficients and tree metadata are data only; seal.py compares every included Python blob against all blobs in five complete vidigoat trees including e3bae2e.
