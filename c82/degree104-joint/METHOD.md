# Frozen round-10 search method

The construction is Doche's, as stated in PROOF.md. Search is heuristic; only exact frozen-certificate interval replay supplies the bound. Credit: C. Doche supplies the perturbed-polynomial construction (J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux 13 (2001), 103–110). V. Patankar (@vidigoat, #280) supplies the iterated LLL numerator search as well as the weighted-evaluation LLL / closest-vector method, public v3/v4/v5 certificates and coefficient bank. Ricardo Pinho supplies the earlier dual-weighted LLL numerator work [Pin26] (PR #229, [pinned contribution](https://github.com/teorth/optimizationproblems/tree/d27a71ff70b57af6e529e6016f741b5cd7dc6f4d)), joint LP, reduced-cost pricing and residue-class restriction. Grinsztajn and Épi supply earlier factor libraries. Credit snapshots include sources/pr280.json, sources/pr301.json and prior_sources/pr280-final.json.

Round 10 was a warm continuation of round 9, which continued round 8 warm. It inherited round 9's final floating master/dual, 116 denominators and 48 active numerators, a complete bank of 15,103 numerator factors, and 109 archive denominators. The inherited baseline replay is evidence/round9-replay.json. Round 8 had added #280 v5 data. V5 reused four #330 factors; README.md and FACTOR_PROVENANCE.json distinguish earlier local factors and public v3/v4/v5 matches by name and full coefficients. Both superseded standalone round-8 and round-9 certificates are omitted.

The inherited standard alternation searches numerators on the current dual, solves the joint LP with full-grid row generation, freezes exact weights and verifies the certificate, reprices the complete numerator bank after every moved solve, then searches CVP denominators on the new dual and repeats. The grid is the inherited N=524288 half-circle midpoint grid; the LP uses HiGHS simplex. The LP is solved in floating point, then multiplicities are rounded to exact integers at scale 10^12 and exponents to exact dyadic rationals at scale 2^20, with equal-fraction release encoding; the resulting certificate is verified. No floating-grid objective certifies a bound.

Numerator generation uses iterated dual-weighted evaluation-lattice LLL; denominator generation uses degree 104, H=X(X-1)P4 residue restriction, 1024 stratified weighted-evaluation samples, 380-bit precision, integer-Gram LLL (delta=.99), Schnorr–Euchner enumeration (four million nodes, 16 retained points), and up to eight IRLS steps per start. Warm starts and accepted archive factors are retained. SEARCH_SUMMARY.json gives seeds and settings. No pricing or CVP optimality claim is made.

The producer used four numerator workers and two denominator workers, with sequential LP solves and verification; from round 23 complete-bank pricing used independent per-factor multiprocessing with unchanged formulas and ordering. This packaging job runs no search and uses one core. The producer completed 26 alternating rounds, numbered 52–77, and stopped on its wall-time budget while the latest completed rounds still improved. The final certificate-producing solve is rounds/archive-num-77-0; rounds/finished.json names its final restricted master and dual. Only those final files are retained, without the duplicate certificate. Historical source paths refer to the producer archive.

The final certificate has 6 monic degree-104 denominators and 40 numerators: two denominators generated in round 10, one generated in round 9, and three generated in round 8 of this search. There are 13 non-monic numerators. Exact v3/v4/v5 overlaps are zero denominator and five numerator factors for each version, namely P1, P2, P3, P4 and P6. Full provenance for every factor is in README.md, FACTOR_PROVENANCE.json and receipts/certificate-facts.json. None of these provenance labels is a novelty claim.

The separately written (same agent lineage) producer verifier and clean-room referee checker enclose the final bound; their scope and caveats are in PROOF.md and review/. No third-party Python is copied, imported or executed. Public coefficients and tree metadata are data only; seal.py compares every included Python blob against all blobs in four complete vidigoat trees including 191064e.
