# Frozen round-9 search method

The construction is Doche's, as stated in PROOF.md. Search is heuristic; only exact frozen-certificate interval replay supplies the bound. Credit: V. Patankar (@vidigoat, #280), weighted-evaluation LLL / closest-vector search and public v3/v4/v5 coefficient data; Ricardo Pinho (#301/#330), joint LP, reduced-cost pricing and residue restriction; Grinsztajn and Épi, earlier factor libraries.

Round 9 was a warm continuation of round 8, using its final floating master/dual, 115 denominators and 37 active numerators, a complete bank of 14,192 numerator factors, and 109 archive denominators. The inherited baseline replay is evidence/round8-replay.json. Round 8 had added #280 v5 data. V5 reused four #330 factors; README.md and FACTOR_PROVENANCE.json distinguish original local matches and public v3/v4/v5 matches by name and full coefficients. The superseded standalone round-8 certificate is omitted.

The inherited standard alternation searches numerators on the current dual, solves the joint LP with full-grid row generation, freezes exact weights and verifies the certificate, reprices the complete numerator bank after every moved solve, then searches CVP denominators on the new dual and repeats. The grid is the inherited N=524288 half-circle midpoint grid; the LP uses HiGHS simplex. Integer multiplicities are frozen at scale 10^12 and rational exponents at dyadic scale 2^20, with equal-fraction release encoding. No floating-grid objective certifies a bound.

Numerator generation uses iterated dual-weighted evaluation-lattice LLL; denominator generation uses degree 104, H=X(X-1)P4 residue restriction, 1024 stratified weighted-evaluation samples, 380-bit precision, integer-Gram LLL (delta=.99), Schnorr–Euchner enumeration (four million nodes, 16 retained points), and up to eight IRLS steps per start. Warm starts and accepted archive factors are retained. SEARCH_SUMMARY.json gives seeds and settings. No pricing or CVP optimality claim is made.

The producer used four numerator workers and two denominator workers, with sequential LP solves and verification; from round 23 complete-bank pricing used independent per-factor multiprocessing with unchanged formulas and ordering. This packaging job runs no search and uses one core. The producer completed 30 alternating rounds, numbered 22–51, and stopped on its wall-time budget while the latest completed rounds still improved. The final certificate-producing solve is rounds/archive-num-51-1; rounds/finished.json names its final restricted master and dual. Only those final files are retained, without the duplicate certificate. Historical source paths refer to the producer archive.

The final certificate has 5 monic degree-104 denominators and 48 numerators: one denominator generated in round 9, three generated in round 8, and one earlier #330 factor also reused by #280 v5. There are 21 non-monic numerators. Full provenance for every factor, including exact v3/v4/v5 overlaps, is in README.md, FACTOR_PROVENANCE.json and receipts/certificate-facts.json. None of these provenance labels is a novelty claim.

The separately written (same agent lineage) producer verifier and clean-room referee checker enclose the final bound; their scope and caveats are in PROOF.md and review/. No third-party Python is copied, imported or executed. Public coefficients and tree metadata are data only; seal.py compares every included Python blob against all blobs in four complete vidigoat trees including 191064e.
