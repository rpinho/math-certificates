# Construction and theorem credits

Candidate and unrefereed; no novelty judgment.

- Huh–Kim–Oh: four-strand superhelix and long two-bridge constructions
  (2018,2021). Only primary abstracts were available; no full-paper reading
  is claimed. The present geometry and its proof are written explicitly.
- Youhua Li and Claude: unequal coaxial double helix and publicly supplied
  baseline/optimization method (2026), reproduced before this search.
- Olsen–Bohr; Klotz–Maldonado; Kim–Oh–Huh: preceding ropelength constructions
  listed in the sweep and live table. Diao: alternating-knot context.
- Cantarella–Kusner–Sullivan, following Litherland–Simon–Durumeric–Rawdon:
  thickness, tangent-point and reach criteria (full CKS paper reopened).
- Richard L. Bishop: relatively parallel normal frames. Classical
  Frenet–Serret frame formulas are displayed and differentiated explicitly.
- John H. Conway; Louis H. Kauffman and Sofia Lambropoulou: rational tangles
  and the color-fraction classification (full primary KL papers reopened).
- Murasugi, Kauffman and Thistlethwaite: reduced alternating diagram
  crossing minimality. Full original Murasugi/Thistlethwaite papers read;
  access to Kauffman's full journal PDF was unavailable.
- NumPy/SciPy for unverified discovery; python-flint/FLINT/Arb for rigorous
  interval verification. Exact identities use Python Fraction arithmetic.
