## Notable Audit Observations (No Gaps Found)

1. **Implicit $k\ge 2$ and $n=2$:** The manuscript writes "Let $k\le n-1$ be the first index with $\theta_k>\pi/4$" without explicitly noting $k\ge 2$. As proved above, $k=1$ is immediately impossible because $r X_0 = 1/\sqrt{2} > q = q A_0$ (and also violates `(3)` since $s=1\implies t=1-r>(q-1/2)/r$). In particular $n=2$ is already settled by the sector-escape step (giving $R_2\ge 1/\sqrt{2}$).
2. **Precondition $c>r$:** Line 84 of `argument.tex` writes "$q<2c\sqrt{1-c^2}$ and $c>r$". In fact only $0<c<1$ and $q<2c\sqrt{1-c^2}$ are needed for the $\lvert U_j\rvert\ge cV_j$ induction (including obtuse angles, where $2c > 2c\sqrt{1-c^2} > q$ holds for all $c\in(0,1)$); $c=0.9657>r$ holds regardless.
3. **Full $(x',a)$ rectangle non-negativity of radicands:** Because $(x',a)$ is relaxed to a rectangle $[x_-,x_+]\times[a_-,a_+]$, corners can violate $a\ge (x')^2$. Soundness of the $\partial/\partial x'$ monotonicity reduction in Sec. 4.2 therefore requires explicitly certifying $A_1^2(h_-,R_+,x_+,a_-)>0$ and $A_2^2(h_-,R_+,x_+,a_-)>0$ in addition to $A_1^{\max}<2A_2^{\min}$ at $(h_-,R_+)$. Both verifiers enforce strict positivity of both radicands before applying the reduction (or return undecided).
4. **Exact $q$-thresholds for the two analytic preconditions:**
   - $c$-precondition ($c=9657/10000$): $q < 2c\sqrt{1-c^2} = \frac{9657\sqrt{6742351}}{50000000} \approx 0.5015080469896$.
   - Index precondition: $r+\sqrt{q^2-1/4}+1/4 \le 1 \iff q \le \sqrt{21/16-3\sqrt{2}/4} \approx 0.5018364556157$.
   - Both hold with margin at $q=501/1000$ and $q=5001/10000$.
5. **Hand-proof exactness (`check_hand_proof.py`):** Every constant in Section 4.1 (`0.7071<r<0.7072`, `xi=0.00010001`, `sqrt(xi)<0.01001`, `t<0.000142`, `s<0.708`, `I<0.00049`, `b=0.018`, `0.462<v<0.481`, `1.47<R<1.533`, `m_1<0.359`, `L<0.00055`, `0.5<=|mu|^2<0.50021`, `0.489<Re(mu)<0.511`, `E<0.0461`, `q*sqrt(1-c^2)<0.130`, `U_*<0.177`, `A_1>1.379`, `A_2<1.178`, `0.201>0.177`) was checked in exact `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic.
