There is a second definite error in `submission/REFEREE.md:36`: it states that concavity of `J-2 Ent` does not imply concavity of J. With the source's concave Ent this implication *does* hold, since `J=(J-2 Ent)+2 Ent`. The relevant concave quantity in v1 is instead `A=J+2 Ent`. This is a sign error in the packet's diagnosis, not a validation of the withdrawn result.

Our independently written pairing checker tested 5,313 pairs from statistic-fixed classes at input sizes 1 through 5. All were valid balanced dominoes with additive leaf counts, an empty-strip loss at most one per cell, and a two-leaf count increase at most two. One witness has input cut 1 and inputs sigma=(1,2,4,5,3), tau=(1,3,4,5,2). Both have top statistics (leaves,empty,two-leaf)=(2,1,0), bottom=(1,0,0). Their physical concatenation with rotated tau is `(5,7,9,10,8,4,1,2,3,6)`, cut at value 5. Its top strip multiset is {1,1,1}; its bottom strip multiset is {0,1,2}. Thus equality of the two output profiles is false even in a fixed-statistic input class. The candidate's inequalities accommodate both outputs. See `independent_pairing_check.py` and `review_logs/independent_pairing.json`.

The candidate's first-moment extraction also has a materially weaker hypothesis than v2 concentration. It uses only a real derivative ratio below 1/10 and nonnegative coefficients to force divergence of the good family's second derivative at rho. This yields a full-growth limsup subsequence. Polynomial pigeonholing and actual arch concatenation preserve that subsequence; the coefficient lemma supplies a common component-count sequence on it. I found no use of a withdrawn concentration or equal-output-profile assertion in this chain.
