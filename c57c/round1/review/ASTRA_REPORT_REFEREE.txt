External premises and logical direction:
The table's C57c is the infimum of image-disk radii over univalent maps
with f'(0)=1. For globally univalent maps this is exactly the image
inradius; translation of f(0) and rotation do not change it. Thus the
packet studies the correct constant, not the locally univalent or ordinary
Bloch constant.

Landau normalization is valid: restrict to F(rz), maximize its hyperbolic
derivative at an interior point, compose with a disk automorphism and
normalize. The similarity factor M is at least r|F'(0)| and
R_F >= M R_f. The normalized derivative inequality implies a2=0,
|a3|<=1/3 and |f(z)|<=atanh|z|. No Bloch inequality is being assumed for
an arbitrary unnormalized map. These premises match Wikstrom's Lemma 2.1.

For each normalized univalent transform F, F(z)/z is zero-free and has a
holomorphic logarithm on the disk. The exterior odd square root is
holomorphic and univalent, with leading coefficient one: equality of its
values forces equal squared arguments, and the opposite-argument case
is excluded by oddness and nonvanishing. This verifies the hypotheses for
both the exterior area theorem sum n|h_n|^2<=1 and strong Grunsky
contraction. Finite matrix compressions are contractions and therefore
||z||^2+Re(z^T M z)>=0 for every complex z. The sign, square-root index
weights, and complex bilinear (rather than Hermitian) form are correct.

The omitted-value Mobius transform has no pole. The bounded-image
quadratic transform has one omitted pole and its other pole lies outside
the radial image bound. The two-value identity proves injectivity, and
both transforms retain derivative one. The stated t and center bounds
are checked in Arb. The area-derived caps for a5 through a8 have the
correct coefficients and directions. Cauchy's derivative tail on |z|=4/5
is a convergent geometric bound for every chosen radius <=1/4.

The parabolic coefficient map covers all actual tuples after the stated
symmetries. At u=0, choose x2=0; the canceled scaled-Bloch expression is
the valid fixed-function limit plus 2/c^2. Its validity is not needed at
redundant endpoint representations with x2>0. Taylor remainder bounds
majorize actual higher-coefficient contributions; their box bound E is
held fixed when differentiating the core matrix. Negative definiteness
contradicts a nonnegative necessary sum. Moving-phase vectors are allowed
pointwise for every angle and preserve their norm. The covered circle is
inside a simply connected image, so the winding-number argument fills its
interior. No radial monotonicity assumption is needed for this step.

Every part header uses the exact fraction 6123/10000, equal to the exact
terminating decimal 0.61230. Arb encloses this rational and the checker
requires strict exclusion or strict positive definiteness; it does not
accept by rounding a computed lower radius upward. The final transfer is
R_F >= r|F'(0)|R followed by r increasing to one, hence C57c >= R.
The gain over Wikstrom's 0.612242 is exactly 29/500000 = 0.000058.
