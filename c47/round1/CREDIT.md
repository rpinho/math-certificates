# Credit and scope

This construction is further hole-filling on Tomar's measure: the same insertion-and-refinement operation as his hf.py, applied by our own search scripts. Our c47r1 search scripts built the measure from Tejasvi Singh Tomar's published 50-atom measure. The earlier search used a doubled period; the lower half alone gives the better certificate. The construction credit belongs to Tomar's hole-filling method.

Tomar supplies the 50-atom baseline, floating rectangle evaluator, hole-filling method, exact box/radius checker, and the general finite-periodic rectangle result, **Lemma 6.2 following Yongxi Lin**. Lin supplies the alternating weighted lattice and truncation-and-smearing foundation. The general finite-atom formulation should not be attributed to Lin alone.

- Tomar, Tejasvi Singh. *Bounds for the weak type (1,1) constant of the centred maximal operator over squares.* October 2026, preprint, not peer reviewed. [Author page](https://www.mefyi.com/papers/weak-type-squares/), [v5 paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23716077), [supplement](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23716078), [source](https://github.com/tejasvi/square-maximal-weak-type/tree/v5), commit `3af09fc359ce262f4a7f63d34b0d97e6d4d95d1a`.
- Lin, Yongxi. [Weighted lattice and periodic proof source](https://github.com/CoolRmal/centered-maximal-constant/tree/72c022ba09032b522c878cca2f9b6c68217d77ea).

`certificate_scan.c` derives from Tomar's C evaluator, with box export added in c47r1. `make_fast_certificate.py` reconstructs its chosen rectangles exactly. The upstream Apache-2.0 license is retained. `verify_exact.py` is copied byte-for-byte from c47r1, **separately written (same agent)**; it imports no producer or search code. The witness certificate and finite argument are **candidate, unrefereed**. No Lean proof of this candidate was built. The earlier 117-atom packet was reviewed by GPT-6 Astra (ACCEPT) and Claude Opus 5.5 via Claude Science (FLAG; it corrected an atom-pair count). Both then reviewed this release (the 59-atom certificate, proof, table diff and text), and their findings were wording corrections only, concerning the review attribution and the description of the method; those corrections are fixed in this text. Our publish gate requires both reviewers to accept the final bytes; there is no human referee.
