# Method and validation boundary

The proof criterion is unchanged from round3: Smith's Proposition5.15 and Theorem1.5, with positive-log potential, exact mass normalization, and resultants for every nonzero integer polynomial. See PROOF.md. Complex-root and nonmonic auxiliary polynomials are permitted. A candidate numerical LP value is never used as a mathematical bound without the frozen-artifact checkers.

The inherited baseline is a piecewise-linear density. It is exactly a nonnegative combination of unit-mass hats with knots (e[k-1],e[k],e[k+1]) and coefficient h[k]*(e[k+1]-e[k-1])/2. We retain every positive baseline hat as a free variable. Adding any nonnegative local triangular density therefore gives a nested cone containing the exact baseline. Extra directions include previously forbidden gap hats on the source grid, midpoint hats within cells, and quarter-cell hats near a zero baseline height. Their endpoints remain rational. The source is Tomar's adaptive grid, extended/refined in prior rounds; this is not a claim to have invented hat densities.

For a unit-mass triangular hat with l<c<r, its height at c is2/(r-l), mean is(l+c+r)/3, and logarithmic potential is the slope-jump sum of H(u)=u^2(log|u|/2-3/4). A stable far-field moment series is used only in floating search. The export adds all selected hats exactly on the union of rational knots. Exact rational mass and first moment determine the declared ratio T/m. Auxiliary weights are rounded down to multiples1e-14; masses are rounded to multiples1e-14. Verifiers reconstruct the final density from its node data rather than accepting the search's formula or normalization.

Candidate directions are priced by c-U^T nu-IL^T gamma-lambda. The inherited potential dual is available; integral/mass duals are reconstructed from positive-density stationarity using constrained least squares, then checked by a residual. This pricing is heuristic and does not prove LP optimality. Subsequent searches save their actual full LP duals. Floating LP constraints use potential margin2e-6, direct auxiliary integrals1e-7, weighted-degree limit1-1e-8 and mass1. A dense scan and continuous local minimization propose added potential cuts. Bounds require exact/Arb continuous coverage after freezing.
