The two regenerable closed covers are omitted. Their exact byte hashes are in ../../sources/omitted-artifacts.json. Regenerate the producer cover from the packet root with `python parallel_verify.py certificate.json --workers 4 --output release_validation/second.json`; regenerate the referee cover from the referee folder with `python cover_check.py ../certificate.json --workers 4 --output ../release_validation/referee-cover.jsonl`. Parallel scheduling changes the referee JSONL order and runtime fields, so a new byte hash may differ while exact audited coverage and minima agree.
