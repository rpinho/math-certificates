**Step 1 — HOLDS (lines 338–353).** The statement “if u takes only integer values, every c_T is an integer” follows from the displayed subset-lattice inversion. **Step 2 — HOLDS (lines 355–362).** The assertion “the bit degree and the real (Fourier) degree of any function on the cube are equal” follows from the degree-nonincreasing substitutions in both directions and uniqueness. The conclusion that g has degree at most d is correct even when f has degree strictly less than d. This does not identify real degree with algebraic degree over F_2; the latter can decrease on reduction modulo two.
