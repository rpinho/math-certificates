Mathematical review of the new reductions

Parity lemma: f must be sign-valued and attain R_d, the maximum number of
relevant variables over ALL dimensions among real-degree-at-most-d Boolean
functions. This is the definition in the packet. For F(x,z)=z^d f(zx), a
Fourier mask S becomes S or S union {z}. In the latter case |S|<=d-1, so
degree remains <=d. Restriction z=1 preserves each old relevant variable.
The new z is relevant exactly when the opposite-parity Fourier sum is
nonzero. Different masks cannot cancel that sum. Thus any such term would
increase the variable count at the same degree bound, a contradiction.
This proves the assertion for every count maximizer. It is not an assertion
about all degree-d functions or maximization within a fixed ambient dimension.
The separate local-degree substitution argument gives d_i=d. At d=7, f is
odd and A=Delta_i f is even, ternary, and of degree at most 6.

Minimum-partial counting: the finite statement is for even ternary A of
degree at most 6, exactly h=21 relevant variables, and lambda=64 E A^2 equal
to 15/8 or 31/16. A constant coefficient is allowed throughout. With integer
Fourier numerators on denominator 64, N=120 or 124, m=32, q=16 and
delta=6N-32h is respectively 48 or 72. For every relevant coordinate,
Q_i=2 Delta_i A is a nonzero integer-valued degree-at-most-5 polynomial.
The gap gives e_i=32 or e_i>=48; evaluation at all plus also gives even e_i.
Consequently at most 3 or 4 coordinates are exceptional, leaving at least
18 or 17 minimum partials. The omitted coordinates need not be exceptional.

For a minimum partial with v_i other relevant coordinates, its classified
tensor gives L_i=32(5-v_i/2). A nonhomogeneous one has L_i>=16. Evenness is
essential to the bound sum_i L_i<=4L: lower masks have sizes at most 4.
If all 21-b minimum partials were nonhomogeneous, then
  16(21-b)<=4(delta-16b), equivalently 336+48b<=4delta.
This is impossible for both delta=48 and delta=72 and every b>=0. A homogeneous
minimum seed therefore exists. Its five pairs and distinguished coordinate
are exactly the frozen seed. No maximality assumption on A is needed beyond
the earlier deduction of evenness from the extremal parent f.

A point block would put both parities in A's Fourier support, so it is
excluded. All seven cost-five block partitions with block sizes at least 2
remain. Selecting 18 or 17 minimum coordinates containing the distinguished
one and quotienting only by seed-pair permutations, pair flips and permutations
outside the seed gives exactly six and nine omitted-coordinate profiles.
The argument permits additional minimum coordinates outside the selected set.

I checked the affine-flat equality proof: support density and integrality
force 32 unit Fourier numerators for each minimum Q_i. On an active input
they all align in sign. Mask differences constrain the active support and
have rank exactly 5. A constant-one projection in another coordinate would,
together with five independent coordinate projections of the direction space,
produce a mask of degree at least 6 in Q_i; hence it is impossible. Each other
used coordinate therefore occurs in exactly 16 masks.

I reviewed the enumeration in verify_partial_a.cpp, its Python metadata
driver and verify_partial_b.py against the proof, and reviewed both inherited
allminimum implementations. For an already used selected coordinate there
are at least 16 forced masks. Rank 5 determines the flat; rank 4 leaves one
additional affine coset; larger rank is impossible. Fresh columns are constant
on the direction space, so they appear together on the new coset. Canonical
fresh labels are valid under permutations separately within selected and
omitted unused pools. When the next selected coordinate is unused, the code
enumerates all seven tensor types, all used subsets and both fresh-pool sizes,
ordering equal-size block roles only to remove permutation duplicates.
No arbitrary binary affine transformation is treated as preserving real degree.

All pruning rules checked are necessary: mask parity/size, no new coefficient
touching a completed coordinate, norm/loss bounds, selected energy at most 32,
and incidence needed to reach every coordinate's valid minimum energy. An
unknown squared numerator contributes at most six times itself to incidence.
Omitted coordinates above energy 32 have the even lower target at least 48.
Signs and omitted-only coefficients are relaxed. The final parity leaf is
counted even if those remaining constraints would be impossible, which is safe
for a zero-leaf exclusion. The proposer's extra cooccurrence filter is absent.

The closed-component rejection is justified without assuming a component is
ternary. Fix all other components to obtain an integer-valued translate B of
the component. For every atom a, B-a is a nonzero degree-at-most-6 polynomial,
so the atom has mass at most 63/64. Integer spacing gives
  Var B >= (1-sum p_a^2)/2 >= 63/4096.
Disjoint-variable components have independent variances. Two nonconstant
components require total numerator norm at least 126; N is 120 or 124. A
proper component whose vertices are all completed cannot acquire a new term;
the other relevant coordinates therefore force a forbidden second component.

The predecessor full-deficit lemma is also valid on its stated k>=4,
nonzero ternary, lambda>=1 hypotheses. I checked the single-exception link
argument, the P/T lower complexes, the k=4 modular substitute for the invalid
dimension-one link inference, and the global sign factorization. The latter
uses a third cube direction to turn affine face signs into affine global
signs; disjoint product components attain independent extrema, forcing one
component and zero constant. In the direct h=22 exclusion, I checked both
exceptional-coordinate cases: the two-exception top-energy sums 104,98,98
cannot be multiples of 16; the one-exception P/T incidence estimates and
the final congruence are correct. The all-minimum case is exhaustively checked.

Independent arithmetic

audit/independent_recurrence.py imports no submitted generator or verifier.
It adapts the previously independent referee method to the two new ceilings.
For each d=5,...,96 it fixes every possible integer h, then checks the first
energy-grid point of each applicability regime. All below-two allowed weights
are enumerated. This differs from the submitted implementations' floor-cell
enumerations. With k=d-1, c=R_k/2^k and
  b=max(1/2,max_{1<=t<=d-2}(1/4+2^(-t-1)+R_t/2^(t+1))),
the budgets used are
  C2=2^d c+h+1-2c lambda,
  C3=2^d c+h-2c lambda+4[b-(c+1/2)/2]
with C3 available only when lambda>1 and 3h>4k lambda, plus
  R_d <= d 2^(d-1)/lambda.
The control ceiling is 2k at lambda=1 and floor(2k lambda-1) otherwise,
with the inherited small-degree exclusions and the new R7 ceilings.
At and above two, for fixed h the ceiling requires lambda>=(h+1)/(2k).
Within either C3 applicability regime the budgets decrease, so its first
grid point suffices. The influence lower construction gives lambda<=d/2.
