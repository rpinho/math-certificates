**Exterior chains.** In a face [D,U], take a saturated chain from ∅ to D excluding D and a saturated chain from U to the full set excluding U. Their union has N−dim(face) members. Every member is comparable with every inside set and with the rest of the chain. Adjoining the members of colour c to any closed local c-family preserves closure and adds exactly their count. This holds with D nonempty and for every order of the fixed bits. On a block grid a face of free shape (u,v,w) with fixed-present counts (i,j,k) has table T(x+i,y+j,z+k). The free coordinates can be permuted within each block.

**Singleton corollary.** Write a≥b≥1, a+b=22. Fix the singleton absent and one element of a block of size at least two alternately absent and present. This gives two 21-cubes, whose *cell windows* overlap even though the physical faces are disjoint. Shorten a when b=1 and shorten b when 2≤b≤10. The reduced shape is respectively (20,1) or (a,b−1), covered above. The shared cells have local ranks 1 through 21 in the first window and 0 through 20 in the second. Indeed the shared grid after shifting the origin is a box of total dimension 20. Every such rank occurs. All 16 ordered suffix/prefix comparisons fail (eight distances 10, eight 11). Thus ten singleton splits admit no value-13 colouring. The eleventh, (11,11,1), requires the missing (11,10) classification for this argument. Free z holds, full split coverage does not.

**General face induction.** For ambient N, target h, and free shape of dimension d, admissibility means H_0≤h−z and H_1≤h−(N−d−z). Fixing a free coordinate absent adds the parent top to the exterior, fixing it present adds the parent bottom. Each is strictly comparable with the entire corresponding child. Thus the two child contexts increase z by the indicator that the parent top, respectively bottom, is zero. In any axis of positive size, both windows must belong to the corresponding child lists. This condition may be intersected across axes. To rule out a remaining table it suffices to supply a monochrome closed family of at least h+1, padded with canonical exterior prefixes on N coordinates. Move its interior into the real face and substitute equally many exterior sets of its colour on the real chain. Binary closure and cardinality are preserved.

## Completeness of the three-block finder and the rank upper-bound checker

For a finite sublattice L of a Boolean lattice put m=∩L and M=∪L. Each coordinate x in M∖m labels the least member containing x. This member is join-irreducible because in a binary union equal to it a summand contains x. Every nonbottom join-irreducible j occurs, by taking x in j outside its unique lower cover. Birkhoff's theorem identifies the members with the ideals of these irreducibles ordered by inclusion. The set for an ideal consists of m together with every coordinate labelled by an element of that ideal. An irreducible is join-prime by distributivity, so forming this union introduces precisely the prescribed labels. Conversely disjoint nonempty bundles of coordinates labelled by poset elements realise every such ideal family. For three blocks the base and each bundle have three nonnegative coordinate counts. Base plus all weights must fit componentwise. Distinct ideals may have the same count vector, but give distinct sets.

The components of the incomparability graph form an ordinal sum: a vertex outside a component compares in one direction with both ends of every incomparability edge, hence uniformly with the component. In each connected component a linear extension with connected prefixes exists. From a nonempty proper ideal prefix, if every remaining minimal element lay above every prefix element, transitivity would separate the two sides of the graph. Add a remaining minimal element incomparable with some prefix element. Its predecessor set is an ideal other than the entire prefix. Extending by this element leaves all old ideals and adds exactly the old ideals containing that predecessor set, augmented by the new label. Initially the empty predecessor ideal is allowed.

The finder tries every eligible predecessor ideal and every positive-total vector weight that fits, at every base of the desired colour. Members use initial segments of each coordinate block at component endpoints. If even one new ideal has the wrong colour, it remains an ideal in every further linear extension, justifying that prune. Otherwise all new ideals are counted. The component from s to t attaches to a prefix family ending at s with exactly one common set. Every prefix member lies below every component member. The longest prefix at s therefore suffices. Increasing coordinate index is a topological order on endpoints. These facts prove that threshold search detects any cardinality **at least** the target, and exhaustion gives the true maximum even if the target cardinality is skipped. Input threshold greater than the cube size forces exhaustion. For a rank table, forgetting the blocks leaves exactly the same colouring on the same physical coordinates. Scalar queries and their concrete subset witnesses transfer without change.

The earlier rank-tree verifier exhausts *all* scalar weighted posets, including ordinal sums directly. For every allowed base, it reconstructs the ideals from the predecessor relation by testing every subset. For every ideal I and every positive weight that fits, it requires either a checked forbidden rank on a newly created ideal or the complete child subtree. Every poset has a linear extension, and old ideals remain ideals at later steps. The verifier rejects any reached state with ideal count greater than **or equal to** the bound. This comparison detects jumps past the threshold. The two certified no-at-15 trees and physical 14-families for the 23-dimensional rank word therefore certify maxima 14 and 14. A Boolean 3-cube has no seven-member sublattice: deleting its bottom or top loses a binary meet or join, deleting an atom loses the meet of its two covering coatoms, and deleting a coatom loses the join of its two atoms. Its eight-member lattice still exceeds seven.

## Classification in dimension 21

The catalogue includes every (a,b,c) with a≥b≥c≥0 and a+b+c=21. Axis order costs no generality because relabelling coordinates preserves cardinalities, unions and intersections. The earlier two-block lemma supplies the baseline. The new computation regenerates all two-block classifications including (11,10). It also covers all 37 positive three-block shapes. Neither colour swap nor complement is used to discard tables.

For a positive-dimensional parent choose an axis. List all ways to glue the lower and upper child windows on that axis, requiring equality on the common cells. Use the parent top and bottom colours to select the requisite child exterior counts. A width-one axis may give disjoint windows, in which case every admissible pair is tried. At dimension zero start from both colours in each possible context. The axis is only a choice of enumeration order. To keep an overlap, in a positive three-block shape we shorten the last axis of width at least two, using the third axis at (1,1,1). In a two-block shape we shorten the second axis when it is at least two or the shape is (1,1). In other cases we shorten the first.

There is also a necessary condition on each two-dimensional slice of a three-block table. Fix the count on axis j at k, realising the slice by fixing k bits present and the rest of that block absent. For this slice, add the parent cells with count below k and both other counts zero, and those with count above k and both other counts maximal. These sets can be realised as one chain outside the slice and inside the parent. These sets are strictly comparable with the entire slice. The extra zero count on this chain determines the slice context when added to the parent z. An admissible table must pass every such test against the already derived two-block catalogue.

Every other rejection in the induction has an explicit physical family. The checker verifies its masks, distinctness, cardinality, every binary union and intersection, and the colours of canonical exterior prefixes. It recomputes the local cell image by splitting each actual mask into the indicated coordinate blocks. A single-colour completion of that image in the matching context is impossible by the exterior-chain argument. For local-only families both monochrome assignments are forbidden. Induction proves that the resulting lists contain all admissible tables. At dimension 21 positive admissibility is checked by exhaustive scalar search for both maxima of each remaining rank word.

For the terminal SAT query one of three context variables must hold. Each active context chooses a lower and an upper child table, each belonging to at least one of the two possible child contexts. Binary clauses prohibit pairs inconsistent on common cells, inconsistent with either endpoint-dependent budget, or equal on gluing to a listed table at z=1. Thus any exceptional admissible table would extend to a satisfying assignment by selecting its actual context and child tables. A physical rejection from the SAT loop, when needed, can forbid its pair. Across these 48 terminal instances the joins themselves already forced the four words, with zero exceptional models. Root LRAT proofs for the face queries and for the split exclusions depend on the checked witness induction. The checker re-enumerates that induction using integer tables and bit masks of possible contexts, with no finder or producer imports.

## The 23- and 22-dimensional conclusions

With all 48 shapes classified, the missing (11,10) singleton case follows exactly as above by shortening one of the 11-blocks. For each of the 33 positive splits with minimum at least two, take the largest axis, fix two of its elements and consider offsets 0 and 1. Its original length is at least eight. Each 21-window must carry one of the four words. Their common cells, after subtracting the unit shift, form a box of total dimension 20. Hence all 21 comparisons w[k+1]=v[k] for 0≤k≤20 would be necessary. They cannot hold for any ordered pair of the four words. For each full split, the root DIMACS has actual grid variables, one disjunction of four selectors for each window and implications from a selector to its word on every window cell. Every putative value-13 table extends to a satisfying assignment. All 44 formulas are UNSAT. The rank word with colour maxima 14 and 14 attains the bound on every split.

In dimension 22 take one element of the largest block and fix it absent. If both global maxima were ≤12, both 21-face maxima would be ≤12, exactly the local condition at ambient 23 and z=1. This application applies directly to the two local maxima. Every listed word has a 12-family of each colour. Pick the family of the colour of the full 22-set and adjoin that full set, giving 13. Each root formula uses selectors forcing the four possible words on the facet and physical clauses from eight explicit 13-families, consisting of each of the two local witnesses for each word with the full set adjoined. Every such family forbids both monochrome assignments on its footprint. This excludes value 12 in all positive two- and three-block splits and in the rank control. The 22-dimensional rank construction attains 13 in both colours.
