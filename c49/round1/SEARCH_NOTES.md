# Exact search region and checker reasoning

The frozen target is in BAR.json. The capacity definition and the operative chain formula are preserved verbatim in sources/49a.md; the full gluing statement is preserved verbatim in sources/koshta_FORMAT.md. The live page states the chain rule but does not spell out the general gluing lemma. The mathematical input used throughout is DEGKM1997 Lemma 4.4, followed by the IRSI positive transfer-matrix argument. Verification of a finite family does not prove that published mathematical input from scratch.

Our certificate checker is separately written (same agent), not independent. It reads only the complete mask family, n and the ports. It partitions masks by their low eight coordinates; within each slice, the next ten coordinates are grouped by identical remaining-coordinate fibers. This yields disjoint Cartesian boxes, and equality of the partition's cardinality with the input is checked. No boxes are assumed from the construction.

For pairs of different boxes, a containment exists iff it exists in every factor. Within a box, each factor must itself be an antichain. After this Sperner check, a sunflower with any repeated member necessarily has all three members equal: A=A and A intersect C=A implies A subset C, forcing C=A. Thus, for triples not all in the same disjoint box, existence of a coordinatewise sunflower is exactly existence of a distinct global sunflower. For a triple within one box, we explicitly look for a nonconstant sunflower triple in a factor. Every factor is scanned in literal integer masks, allowing repetitions where appropriate. The resulting global check is exhaustive on the actual family, with no assumption about F/H or the gluing construction.

For a proposed extra mask x and existing pair a,b, the coordinatewise sunflower condition is (a & b) subset x and x disjoint from (a xor b). The possible x form a Boolean interval, which is marked exactly for every pair of boxes and every pair of projected factor collections. Containment intervals are also marked exactly. The complement over all 2^28 masks is the maximal-extension result.

For the deletion screen, any three existing comparable members force at least three deletions. For two disjoint boxes, let E be the number of ordered pair witnesses and D the maximum degree of either endpoint. If E>2D, two deletions cannot remove them all. For one box, E includes both orientations and possible loops; one removed vertex destroys at most 2D pairs, so E>4D is sufficient to exclude two deletions. The count and degrees factor over the two pendant coordinates. This is only a sufficient screen; all survivors receive exact depth-two branching on concrete singleton containment constraints or two-endpoint sunflower constraints. Branching on both endpoints exhausts all deletion covers of size at most two. Zero survivors therefore excludes every move introducing any nonmember after at most two removals, including the requested 1-out-2-in and 2-out-3-in exchanges. It does not exclude larger exchanges.

Coupled search: the complete template domain has all 256 core masks with either an empty part, an H-avoiding-root part (46 choices), or an H-containing-root part with the root removed (75 choices) at each of the two pendants, plus the 21 incumbent exceptional sets. All core product templates and both pendant additions may change simultaneously. We maximize an integer-scaled Perron-gradient objective with CP-SAT, add exact containment conflicts globally, and separate exact sunflower clauses on proposed selected boxes. This is a restricted template search, not an unrestricted global optimum claim. Numerical objective improvements are not certified rate improvements until checker.py accepts the complete expanded masks and exact bar comparison.

The radius-seven union enumeration also needs to account for removals needed only by conflicts of two new members with one old member. For any deletion set R with |R|<=7, choose one minimal individual deletion cover for each introduced candidate, with union U subset R. All introduced candidates lie in C(U). The exact monotone count bound excludes the 147522 nonpromising U. If |U|=7, R=U and the fixed-removal SAT solves exclude improvement. The remaining twelve U have six N00 removals and seven N00 candidates, including a distinct sunflower triple entirely among those seven. Thus at most six can be introduced regardless of any additional old deletion; this cannot increase any port count relative to the incumbent, and an additional old deletion only decreases a count. These twelve regions and all their supersets are excluded. Witness triples are in logs/r7_superset_proof.json. Fixed-removal SAT alone would not justify the radius-seven conclusion.
